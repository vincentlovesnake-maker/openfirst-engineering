# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and respect.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Acknowledge mistakes and work to repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Using project spaces to expose credentials or active vulnerabilities.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for behavior they judge inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct issues through a private repository contact channel. Do not
include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will
respect the privacy and security of reporters as far as reasonably possible.
